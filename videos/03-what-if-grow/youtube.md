The science is real
• 10x taller means 1,000x heavier: your volume grows 10 × 10 × 10.
• Your bones only get 100x stronger: a bone's strength follows the area of its cross-section, 10 × 10.
• So every square inch of bone carries 10x the load. A human thigh bone breaks at about 10 times body weight, so the first step snaps it.
• At 10x her height, the sister's brain sits about 3 m above her heart. Pushing blood that high takes about 235 mmHg, and a child's heart makes about 80: roughly a third of what she needs.
• Without blood, the brain switches off in about 10 seconds (5 to 10 s in the 1943 study below, 6.8 s on average).

• Galileo Galilei, "Two New Sciences" (1638): where the square-cube law comes from
